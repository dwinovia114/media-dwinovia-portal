What is an Advertisement?

An advertisement is a text, picture, audio, or video that contains information about a product, service, event, or idea.

It is designed to attract people's attention and persuade them to take action.

Attract
Persuade
Take Action

Purpose of Advertisement

Why do people create advertisements?

1. To Inform

Give information about a product, service, or event.

2. To Persuade

Convince people to buy or use something.

3. To Promote

Introduce and increase public interest in a product, service, or event.

4. To Remind

Keep a brand or product in people's minds.

Kinds of Advertisement

There are three common forms of advertisement.
Each uses different media to deliver its message.

1. Text & Picture

Combines written words and visual images.

2. Audio

Delivered through sound or spoken words.

3. Video

Combines moving images, sound, and spoken words.

1. Text and Picture Advertisement

Combines written words and images to attract people's attention.

Characteristics

  • Attractive pictures or illustrations
  • Short and interesting sentences
  • Persuasive words
  • Slogans and important information

What are Language Features?

Language features are the specific words, expressions, and sentence patterns commonly used in advertisements. They help advertisers attract attention, communicate messages, and persuade people to take action.

There are five common language features:

1. Persuasive Words 2. Imperative Sentences 3. Simple & Short Sentences 4. Positive Adjectives 5. Questions

1. Persuasive Words

Words used to influence people's thoughts, feelings, or decisions. They make a product sound interesting.

  Common Words

BestThe most excellent
AmazingVery surprising or impressive
DeliciousHaving a very good taste
SpecialDifferent and better than usual
AffordableNot too expensive
FreeWithout payment

  Examples in Advertisements

  • Get the best quality products!
  • Enjoy our amazing chocolate cake!
  • Special discount for students!
  • Get a free gift with every purchase!
  • Don't miss this exclusive offer!
REMEMBER: Persuasive words make people feel interested in a product and encourage them to buy or try it.

2. Imperative Sentences

Sentences used to give commands, instructions, invitations, or suggestions to encourage action.

Characteristics

  • Usually begin with a verb (base form).
  • The subject "you" is hidden.
  • Often end with an exclamation mark (!) or a full stop (.).

Pattern

Verb + Object / Complement
Imperative SentenceFunction
Buy our new product!Encouraging a purchase
Try our delicious burger!Encouraging people to try something
Visit our store today!Inviting customers
Enjoy the fresh taste!Promoting a product
Don't miss this opportunity!Giving a warning or reminder
NEGATIVE FORM: use Don't + base verb — e.g. Don't wait!

3. Simple and Short Sentences

Easier to read, understand, and remember. They deliver the main message quickly.

Simple vocabularyOne main ideaEasy to understandNo unnecessary detailsQuick message
"Our product is made from fresh ingredients and has a very delicious taste."Fresh ingredients. Delicious taste!
"You can enjoy a comfortable experience with our new shoes."Walk comfortably!
"Our drinks are available in many different flavors."More flavors. More fun!
"This product can help you save more money."Save more!

"A good advertisement does not always need long sentences. Sometimes, a few powerful words are enough!"

4. Positive Adjectives

Describing words that show good qualities to make products sound attractive.

FreshNew, clean
DeliciousVery good taste
BeautifulAttractive
ComfortableMakes you feel relaxed
ExcellentExtremely good
ConvenientEasy and useful

Examples

  • Enjoy our delicious chocolate cake!
  • Experience our comfortable shoes!
  • Try our fresh orange juice!

5. Questions

Attract people's attention, make them think, and create curiosity.

Common Expressions

Are you looking for…?Do you want to…?Need a…?Tired of…?

Examples

  1. Are you looking for a comfortable school bag?
  2. Do you want to improve your English?
  3. Tired of boring shoes?
REMEMBER: Questions make the audience feel that the ad is speaking directly to them.
