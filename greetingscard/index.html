Module 01 • Short Functional Text

Greeting Card

Expressing feelings, appreciation, congratulations, sympathy, or good wishes through short written messages.

What is a Greeting Card?

A Greeting Card is a short written message used to express feelings, appreciation, congratulations, sympathy, or good wishes for a particular occasion.

Learning Objectives

Social Function

Identify the social function of Greeting Cards.

Generic Structure

Identify the generic structure of Greeting Cards.

Usage Context

Explain when Greeting Cards are used.

Purpose & Social Function

Express good wishes
Celebrate important events
Show gratitude or sympathy
Send encouragement or support
Maintain positive relationships

When Do We Send Greeting Cards?

Greeting cards are sent on both joyful milestones and moments requiring care.

Graduation

Congratulations

Get Well Soon

Wedding

Thank You

New Baby

Farewell

Sympathy

Holiday Celebrations

Main Types of Greeting Cards

Congratulations Card
Thank You Card
Get Well Soon Card
Sympathy / Condolence Card
Holiday Card
Farewell Card

Generic Structure of a Greeting Card

1

Receiver (Addressee)

The person or group who receives the card (e.g., Dear Rina, or To my best friend,).

2

Body / Message

The main content expressing feelings, appreciation, prayers, or good wishes.

3

Sender (Signature)

The person or group sending the card (e.g., Best wishes, Class IX A).

Sample Analysis

How the Structure Works in Practice

Look at the greeting card example. Notice how clear, warm, and concise the message is—starting with the Receiver, followed by a celebratory Body, and ending with the Sender.

"Keep spreading kindness with simple messages."
1. Receiver

Congratulations!

You Did It!

Dear Rina,

2. Body / Message

Congratulations on winning the competition! We are very proud of you. Keep inspiring everyone.

3. Sender

Best wishes,

Module 02 • Social Expression

Expression of Congratulations

Celebrate Success, Share Happiness! Learn how to express pride, joy, and appreciation for others' achievements.

Definition & Purpose

What is It?

An expression of congratulations is used to show happiness, appreciation, and support for someone's success or happy occasion.

Celebrate Someone's success
Appreciate Someone's achievement
Show Joy Share happiness
Encourage Keep them motivated
Language Focus

Grammar Patterns

Pattern 1: Followed by a Noun

Congratulations + on + Noun

  • • Congratulations on your graduation.
  • • Congratulations on your success.
Pattern 2: Followed by Verb-ing

Congratulations + on + Verb(-ing)

  • • Congratulations on winning the game.
  • • Congratulations on passing the exam.

Short & Casual

  • Congratulations!
  • Well done!
  • Good job!
  • Awesome!

Longer & Formal

  • Congrats on your achievement.
  • We're so proud of you.
  • You really deserve it.
  • Keep up the good work!

Polite Responses

  • "Thank you!"
  • "Thanks a lot."
  • "I really appreciate it."
  • "That's very kind of you."
Conversation Practice

Example Dialogue

Rafi Hey! Congratulations on winning the English Speech Competition! 🎉✨
Nadia Omg, thank you very much! 😄
Rafi You really deserved it. Your speech was amazing! 🔥
Nadia Thanks a lot. I'm very happy right now! 😊
Lesson Summary

Remember!

  • Use expressions of congratulations for happy occasions and achievements.
  • Choose an expression that matches the situation (casual for close friends, formal for teachers or elders).
  • Always respond politely with gratitude when someone congratulates you.
Congratulations = Celebrate someone's happiness! 🎉
Module 03 • Empathy & Care

Expression of Condolences

"Showing Sympathy and Caring for Others" — Learn how to comfort friends and family during moments of sorrow and loss.

📝 Meaning

An expression of condolence is used to show:

  • ❤️ Sympathy
  • ❤️ Sadness
  • ❤️ Care & Support

To someone who is experiencing loss or difficult times.

🌟 Social Function

We use expressions of condolence to...

  • Express sympathy
  • Comfort someone
  • Show empathy
  • Support people in hard times

When Do We Use Them?

We usually say expressions of condolence when someone experiences sad or tragic moments:

🕊️ Loses a family member
🤕 Has an accident
🔥 Experiences a house fire
🌊 Becomes a flood victim
🐱 Loses a beloved pet
🏥 Is seriously ill / hospitalized
💔 Experiences any great loss or hardship

💬 Simple Expressions

  • ✓ I'm sorry to hear that.
  • ✓ I'm so sorry.
  • ✓ I'm sorry for your loss.
  • ✓ Please accept my deepest sympathy.

🕊️ Longer & Heartfelt Expressions

  • ✓ My thoughts are with you.
  • ✓ My heart goes out to you.
  • ✓ May you find strength during this difficult time.
  • ✓ May Allah give you patience.

How to Respond Politely

✨ Thank you.
✨ Thank you for your kindness.
✨ I appreciate your support.
✨ Thank you for your prayers.
✨ That means a lot to me.

Situation & Expression Reference Table

Situation Appropriate Expression

Comforting a Grieving Friend

Supporting a Flood Victim
